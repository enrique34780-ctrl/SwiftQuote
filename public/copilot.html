What the copilot creates

  • Executive summary, goal, scope, and assumptions.
  • Workstreams with tasks, dependencies, and effort notes.
  • Milestones with acceptance criteria.
  • Risk register with severity and mitigations.
  • Deliverables, resources, and budget guidance.
  • Verification steps and the next practical actions.
Boundaries: the copilot plans; it does not silently purchase products, hire providers, deploy software, change accounts, or claim to have completed real-world work. Prices, inventory, permits, dimensions, and external facts must be verified.
