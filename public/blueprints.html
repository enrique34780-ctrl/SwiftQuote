Plan safeguards

A useful plan separates what is known from what still needs checking.

  • Assumptions and missing information are called out.
  • Materials and quantities are planning estimates, not verified purchase quantities.
  • Structural, electrical, plumbing, waterproofing, fire, and accessibility requirements need appropriate professional review.
  • Permit requirements depend on the actual location and scope.
  • No AI-generated plan can guarantee zero errors or replace stamped construction documents.
